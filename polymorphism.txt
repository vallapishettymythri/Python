Polymorphism-
Same operations different implementations.
Ability to take more than one form. 
Example- 
1. Method overloading- We have same method name, but performs different types of operations. 
So method overloading isn't possible in python.

2. Operator Overloading- Same operator but does different tasks. 
2+3- integer addition
"abc"+"pqr"- concatenation
[2,3,4]+[5,6,7]-merging
