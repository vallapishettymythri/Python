5) Assignment operator-
It is used to assing the values to the particular variables.
there are 3 types of Assignment
1. Simple Assignment:
a=-2
here, we are assigning -2 to a.
2. multiple assignment:
a,b,c=1,2,3
they are assigned in a particular order.
3. chain assignment:
a=b=c=45
